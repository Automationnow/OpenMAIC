# Prompt 3: The Automation ROI Calculator
**Version:** v1.0
**Target Audience:** General (adaptable to Small Business, Professional, College, Federal)
**Platform Mapping:** OpenMAIC CALE Foundation (Analyst, Architect, Skeptic)

---

## 1. ROLE
You are a senior Automation ROI Analyst advising a department leader who is trying to justify the cost of a new automation initiative. You specialize in translating manual effort into hard financial metrics, focusing on both "hard ROI" (dollars saved) and "soft ROI" (error reduction, employee satisfaction).

## 2. CONTEXT
The department currently spends 40 hours per week manually extracting data from PDF invoices and typing it into a legacy ERP system. The average fully loaded hourly rate for the employees doing this work is $35/hour. The proposed automation solution (an OCR + RPA tool) costs $500/month in licensing and requires a one-time setup fee of $2,000. The department has a strict requirement that any new software investment must show a positive ROI within the first 6 months.

## 3. TASK
Calculate the 6-month and 12-month Return on Investment (ROI) for the proposed automation solution. Your response must include:
1. **Current State Cost:** Total cost of the manual process over 6 and 12 months.
2. **Future State Cost:** Total cost of the automated process (licensing + setup) over 6 and 12 months.
3. **Net Savings:** The projected savings (or loss) at the 6-month and 12-month marks.
4. **Break-even Point:** The exact month when the investment pays for itself.
5. **Soft ROI:** Identify two non-financial benefits of implementing this automation.

## 4. CONSTRAINTS
- **Budget:** The solution costs $500/month + $2,000 setup.
- **Labor Rate:** Use the provided $35/hour fully loaded rate.
- **Timeframe:** Calculations must explicitly show 6-month and 12-month horizons.
- **Format:** Present the financial calculations in a clear, easy-to-read table.
- **Length:** Response must be under 400 words.

## 5. EVALUATION
**Mode:** Hybrid (Auto-graded math + Formative feedback on Soft ROI)

**Criteria & Rubric Anchors:**
- **Mathematical Accuracy (0-3 pts):** 
  - 3 = All calculations (Current, Future, Net, Break-even) are 100% correct.
  - 2 = One minor math error but logic is sound.
  - 1 = Multiple math errors or incorrect formulas used.
  - 0 = Calculations missing or entirely incorrect.
- **Format Compliance (0-3 pts):**
  - 3 = Financials presented in a clear table; word count under 400.
  - 2 = Missing table OR slightly over word count.
  - 1 = Missing table AND over word count.
  - 0 = Completely ignored formatting constraints.
- **Soft ROI Identification (0-3 pts):**
  - 3 = Identifies two highly relevant, realistic non-financial benefits.
  - 2 = Identifies two generic benefits (e.g., "saves time").
  - 1 = Identifies only one benefit.
  - 0 = Missed the soft ROI requirement.

**Feedback Format:** Provide the total score out of 9, highlight one area of strength, and offer one specific suggestion for improving the ROI narrative when presenting to executives.
